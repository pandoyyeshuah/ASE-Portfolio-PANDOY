# Course Introduction

**CPELX230-330 - Term 2, 2026-2027**
College of Engineering, National University (Computer Engineering)

## Overview
This course introduces modern software engineering practice in a world where AI agents are becoming part of both the software we build and the way we build it. It combines established software engineering foundations with the new concerns raised by autonomous, AI-powered systems.

## Topics covered in Weeks 02-04
- **Agentic Software Engineering:** how AI agents are designed, built, and integrated into software.
- **Agile and Scrum:** Scrum theory, roles, and events for iterative, collaborative development.
- **Version control:** Git basics and collaborative workflows on GitHub.
- **Intelligent agents:** the agent model (perception, reasoning, action) from *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*.
- **Responsible AI:** risk, governance, safety, privacy, and ethics, guided by the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

## Learning objectives
1. Explain the role of Agentic Software Engineering in modern software development.
2. Differentiate traditional software systems from AI-powered agentic systems.
3. Describe the importance of Agile development, version control, and collaborative software engineering.
4. Recognize ethical, security, and responsible AI considerations in developing agentic systems.

## Required readings
- *Software Engineering* by Ian Sommerville, Chapters 1 and 2 (10th Edition)
- The Scrum Guide (2020)
- *Pro Git* (2nd Edition), Chapters 1 and 2
- *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* by Russell and Norvig, Chapter 2
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework (Introduction, AI Risks, Govern Function)
