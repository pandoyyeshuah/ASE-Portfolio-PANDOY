# Personal Learning Goals

1. **Understand agentic systems.** Be able to explain how an AI agent perceives, decides, and acts, and how that differs from a traditional program.
2. **Work confidently with Git and GitHub.** Commit often with clear messages, use branches and pull requests, and keep my repositories organized and accessible.
3. **Apply Agile practices.** Use Scrum ideas (sprints, reviews, retrospectives) in my group projects to plan, deliver, and improve in small steps.
4. **Build responsibly.** Consider safety, security, privacy, and bias from the start of a project, and keep a human in the loop for important decisions.
5. **Connect software engineering to my other fields.** Apply what I learn to embedded systems and machine learning projects, where software interacts with hardware and real data.
6. **Communicate clearly.** Document my work well enough that teammates and instructors can understand, run, and review it.
